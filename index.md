<p>I am Taewook [teug] Kim, a Postdoctoral Scholar at the <a href="https://datascience.uchicago.edu/people/taewook-kim/" target="_blank">University of Chicago Data Science Institute (DSI)</a>. I work at the intersection of human-computer interaction (HCI) and AI. I received my Ph.D. from Northwestern University, where I was advised by <a href="https://www.mjskay.com/" target="_blank">Matt Kay</a>. During my Ph.D., I was a research intern at <a href="https://www.accenture.com/en/about/accenture-innovation" target="_blank">Accenture Labs</a>, <a href="https://mj-storytelling.github.io/" target="_blank">Midjourney</a>, and <a href="https://www.tri.global/our-work/human-centered-ai" target="_blank">Toyota Research Institute</a>.</p>

<p>Many great ideas begin with a hunch or a half-formed thought that is hard to put into words. People often turn to generative AI hoping it will complete the other half and add a spark. Instead, it fills the gaps with the most likely completions, producing results that look polished but are often clichéd. This polish makes it hard for people to tell whether they like an output because it reflects their intent or simply because it looks great.</p>

<p>My research has focused on designing and building interfaces that let people explore beyond these clichés and refine their own intent. I am now extending this work to study model behavior and internals, making AI more reliable and steerable. My goal is to accelerate creative and scientific work while keeping people as the primary actors.</p>

<p>I am always happy to talk about these or related topics. Feel free to reach out at first_name at uchicago dot edu, or <a href="https://calendar.app.google/sH1DgExeLFnRLWRj9" target="_blank">schedule a chat</a> with me.</p>
